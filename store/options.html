Replace fan identities on your screen without changing accounts, sending messages, or transmitting fan information to the developer.

Before masking starts

To create consistent aliases, the extension reads names, handles, profile images, and stable account or chat identifiers already visible in loaded OnlyFans pages. It stores identifiers, aliases, avatar assignments, and your settings only in Chrome local extension storage. No fan information is transmitted to the developer or another service.
